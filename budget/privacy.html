Budget privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Budget is a household budgeting app for one family's private use. This policy says what data the app handles, where it keeps it, and what it does with access to Google Drive.

Data the app handles

Bank transactions imported from statement files, the categories and budgets the family sets, and photos of receipts.

Where the data is kept

  • On the family's PC. The financial data is encrypted; receipt photos are kept as ordinary image files.
  • On the family's phones, encrypted.
  • In the family's own Google Drive, as files the app creates. Each file is encrypted on the device before it is uploaded, and the key never leaves the family's devices, so Google stores only data it cannot read.

Google user data

The app asks for one Google permission, the Google Drive scope drive.file. It gives the app access only to files the app created or that the user picked in the Google Drive file picker. The app cannot see, list or change any other file in the user's Drive. It uses this access only to store its own data files and to read them on the family's other devices.

Budget's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

The app runs no server, contains no analytics or advertising, and sends no data to the developer or to anyone else. Its data moves only between the family's own devices and their own Google Drive.

Removing data

Delete the app's files from Google Drive and uninstall the app. Its access to Google Drive can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date.
